## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury spiritual travel experience tailored for pilgrims embarking on Umrah journeys from Spain. The aesthetic blends nocturnal tranquility, sacred architectural grandeur, and modern European hospitality. The visual atmosphere is calm, reverent, and sophisticated, evoking the profound serenity of a clear desert night sky and luminous pilgrimage sanctuaries.

The core design philosophy merges refined **Minimalism** with atmospheric **Glassmorphism**:
- Deep midnight backdrops create a private, quiet sanctuary where content breathes effortlessly.
- Luminous cyan accents evoke high-altitude travel vectors, directional clarity, and spiritual enlightenment.
- Translucent frosted glass layers provide depth without visual noise, mirroring the layered domes and reflective marble surfaces of holy sites.
- Editorial serif headings in wide-tracked uppercase impart timeless heritage, while clean humanist sans-serifs guarantee effortless booking, flight schedules, and itinerary legibility.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in a dark celestial theme with maritime and desert night depth:

- **Canvas & Background (`#031737`):** Deep nocturnal sapphire, establishing supreme contrast, calm ambiance, and ocular rest for long reading and planning sessions.
- **Glass Surfaces & Containers (`#13324F` at 70% opacity):** Frosted oceanic slate, functioning as the primary container hue across cards, navigation panels, and interactive flyouts with a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` hairline boundary.
- **Primary Action (`#014F88`):** Deep royal Mediterranean azure, used for structural focal points, active states, key buttons, and prominent navigation accents.
- **Luminous Accent (`#30BBD8`):** Radiant cyan, utilized selectively for flight path trajectories, glowing indicators, booking status pills, call-to-action highlights, and pricing accents.
- **Heritage & Historical Accents (`#123435` and `#375F59`):** Deep and tempered petroleum greens reserved for historical storytelling, legacy badges, spiritual guides, and cultural context sections.
- **Text & Foreground:** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary titles, monetary values, and high-priority data; muted mist cyan-gray (`#B4C1C9`) for secondary labels, flight timings, and long-form prose.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances ceremonial grandeur with functional precision:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Styled primarily in uppercase or elegant small-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury editorial mastheads and monument inscriptions.
- **Data & Commercial Points (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for package prices, flight numbers, duration counters, and interactive affordances. Its geometric sharpness delivers immediate legibility when scanning complex travel packages.
- **Narrative Body (`Inter`):** Neutral, humanist clarity optimized for itineraries, visa guidelines, terms, and theological historical essays.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid underpins desktop viewports, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet devices, and a structured 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm (`space-xl` between modular itinerary chapters) replicates high-end travel journals and avoids density overload.
- **Edge Containment:** Maximum desktop canvas is constrained to 1280px with centered auto-margins, maintaining optimal reading widths for sacred texts and tour schedules.
- **Fluid Flight Paths:** SVG flight curves weave diagonally through gutters across card breakpoints, visually connecting Madrid/Barcelona departure columns to Medina/Mecca arrival cards without overlapping interactive targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal layers rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#031737` canvas augmented by subtle radial gradients (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 80% 0%, rgba(48, 187, 216, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).
- **Glassmorphic Tier 1 (Cards & Modules):** `background: rgba(19, 50, 79, 0.70)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, encapsulated by an ultra-thin perimeter stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`. Soft diffuse shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(1, 23, 55, 0.6)`.
- **Glassmorphic Tier 2 (Floating Nav, Dialogs & Modals):** `background: rgba(19, 50, 79, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, `1px solid rgba(48, 187, 216, 0.25)`, elevated by a double glow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(48, 187, 216, 0.15)`.
- **Interactive Lift:** On hover, travel package cards subtly translate up `-4px`, with border luminescence shifting to `rgba(48, 187, 216, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometric contours:
- Standard elements (inputs, inner chips, sub-cards) utilize `rounded-md` (0.5rem).
- Travel packages, modal containers, and hero preview windows utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) to evoke soft glass slabs.
- Status badges and primary CTAs use soft pill geometry (`rounded-full`) to complement the flight curves and arc motifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Gradient background from `#014F88` to `#0B6FB3` with a glowing `#30BBD8` hover transition. Text in white uppercase `label-caps`. Internal padding `space-sm` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal.
- **Secondary (Glass):** Transparent base with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` surface, `1px solid rgba(48, 187, 216, 0.3)` border, and cyan text `#30BBD8`.
- **Ghost:** Pure text with a subtle underline effect that expands on hover using `#30BBD8`.

### Flight & Package Cards
- **Structure:** Card chassis crafted in `bg-[#13324F]/70` with `backdrop-blur-md` and `border border-white/10`.
- **Header:** Features departure city (e.g., "MADRID - MAD") to destination ("JEDDAH / MEDINA") connected via a delicate curved SVG line dotted in `#30BBD8`.
- **Pricing:** Highlighted in bold `price-display` using `#FFFFFF` with package duration tags styled in petroleum green (`bg-[#123435]` with border `#375F59`).

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Dark field surface `rgba(3, 23, 55, 0.6)` with an inset hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Focus state activates a subtle outer cyan aura: `border-color: #30BBD8; box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(48, 187, 216, 0.2)`. Placeholder color set to `#B4C1C9` at 60% opacity.

### Badges & Chips
- Pilgrimage type tags ("Umrah Ramadán", "Umrah Grupo", "VIP Deluxe") configured with `bg-[#375F59]/30`, border `1px solid #375F59`, text `#FFFFFF`, and `border-radius: 9999px`.

### Flight Curves & Trackers
- Curving bezier vector tracks styled with stroke width `1.5px`, stroke color `#30BBD8`, with a micro glowing plane marker at transit intervals.
